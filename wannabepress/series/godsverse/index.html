The Godsverse Chronicles

13 novels + 4 comics · Mythic portal fantasy

Every time a new world forms, every pantheon shows up to make it livable. Then the gods move on and leave one of their own behind as regent. Some rule well. Others downright suck.

Across the series the characters travel to Heaven, Hell, Mount Olympus, Valhalla, other planets and other dimensions, through Greek, Roman, Norse, Egyptian and Judeo-Christian mythology. It’s fast-paced and full of plot twists.
