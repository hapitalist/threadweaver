Ichabod Jones: Monster Hunter

4 comics + a story RPG · Horror comedy

Ichabod wakes up in the Apocalypse with a monster trying to devour his soul. He breaks a band of inmates out of the asylum and leads them across the desert, never sure if he’s killing monsters, humans, or if it’s all in his head.

Think Lovecraft’s Dreamlands, only funnier and gorier. A complete fantastical and psychological mindscrew. There’s also a card game, made with the German publisher Wredespiele.
