Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Thread Weaver ("the app") is published by Wannabe Press Limited Liability Corporation ("we," "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and password, used to create and secure your account.
  • Reading activity: your place in books and which titles you open, so your library and progress sync across your devices.
  • Purchases: records of which content you have unlocked, so your purchases are restored on any device where you sign in.

How we use it

We use this information solely to operate the app: to sign you in, sync your reading position, deliver the content you own, and provide support. We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Payments

Purchases made inside the app are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their respective terms. Purchases made on the web are processed by Stripe. We receive a record of what you purchased, but we do not receive or store your full payment-card details.

Data storage

Account, reading, and entitlement data are stored using Supabase, our backend provider, on secured servers. Access is protected and limited to operating the app.

Your choices

  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time. See our account deletion page.
  • You may contact us with any privacy question at help@wannabepress.com.

Children

Thread Weaver is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new "last updated" date.
