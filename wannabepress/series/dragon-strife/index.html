Dragon Strife

5 books + a story RPG · YA epic fantasy

Gilda has lived her whole life for one purpose: to be sacrificed to the great dragon lord Ewig. The city gave her riches and the finest food, and everyone resented her for it. Tonight she walks up the volcano to the dragon’s keep.

Dragon sacrifice stories usually belong to the hero who saves the damsel. This one asks what happens when the sacrifice becomes the hero, and what the people society casts out can do to change the world. The story continues in Wicked Bodies and Wicked Minds.
