The Marked Ones

Novel · YA · Fantasy

“Magic destroyed the world once. Now, magic users are the enemy.”

Magic destroyed the world once. Now, magic users are the enemy. To be marked with magic is to lead a cursed life.

Rosie is completely average. There is absolutely nothing extraordinary about her in any way. For most people, that would be a blessing, but Rosie wants nothing more than to have a spark of magic, even if people say that all mages are evil.

So, when she’s suddenly able to wield magic, Rosie is initially confused, then thrilled…until the soldiers come for her, and she realizes that her life is over…at least the life she knew.

Now, she must escape to the lost city of Toledo – a haven for witches and warlocks since before the war, and the only place Rosie can be free.

But nobody has seen the city in decades. Can she find the lost city before the army descends upon her?
