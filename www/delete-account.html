Account & Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Thread Weaver account and all associated data at any time. This page explains what gets deleted and how to ask for it.

Fastest: in the Thread Weaver app, sign in and tap Delete account in the top bar — the whole deletion completes in the app, no email needed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email help@wannabepress.com from the email address on your account.
  2. Put Delete my account in the subject line.
  3. We will confirm and permanently delete your account within 30 days.

Deleting specific data without closing your account

You may also request deletion of specific data (for example, your reading annotations, bookmarks, or progress) without deleting your account. Email help@wannabepress.com from the email address on your account and tell us which data to remove; we will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your account and login (email address and credentials)
  • Your reading progress and saved positions
  • Your purchase and entitlement records held in our system
  • Any other personal data tied to your account

What may be retained

We may retain a minimal record of completed transactions where required for tax, accounting, or legal obligations. This data is not used to identify you for any other purpose and is held only as long as the law requires.
