About Russell

I’m Russell Nohelty, a USA Today bestselling author of fast-paced action-adventure fantasy with a lot of humor. I’ve written 45+ novels plus comics, nonfiction and children’s books, and I run Wannabe Press, a publisher that makes weird books for weird people.

I’ve raised millions for my own creative projects through 50+ Kickstarter campaigns. I co-founded Writer MBA and the Future of Publishing Mastermind, co-created the Author Ecosystems, and co-host the Six Figure Author Experiment podcast with Lee Savino.

I live in Los Angeles, and I’m still a little proud I was co-editor-in-chief of my high school yearbook.
