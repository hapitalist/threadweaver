The Obsidian Spindle Saga

12 novels + a story RPG · YA portal fantasy

Rose Briar can’t afford her insulin. She falls into a coma and wakes in the Dream Realm, where every fairy tale is alive and the Wicked Witch wants her dead. Her only way home is the Obsidian Spindle, one of the most powerful objects in the universe.

Three arcs move from the Dream and Nightmare Realms to the Underworld and the Fairy Realm, then to the Celestial Realm and the Dark Planet, told from viewpoints all across the world of Urgu. Every book ends on a cliffhanger, and you can jump in at Book 1, 5 or 9.
